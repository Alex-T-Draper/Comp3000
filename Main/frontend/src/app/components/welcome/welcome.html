Digital Reading and Interaction Study

Understanding how users read and interact with on-screen documents

Study Information

Thank you for participating in this research study. You will be asked to read through digital documents and complete short mini-games. During the session, I will record interaction and attention data to better understand reading behavior in digital environments.

Estimated time: 15-30 minutes
Eye tracking will be used during reading tasks
Scrolling, clicks, and task interactions will be recorded
Your data will be handled confidentially and used for academic research only

Consent: By entering your name and clicking "Start Study", you consent to participate in this research and have your name and session data recorded for analysis, including eye-tracking signals, scrolling behavior, clicks, and task performance.
